Rich-text field spike

Enter splits, Shift+Enter breaks the line, Backspace at the start merges. Ctrl/Cmd+B, I, U, Shift+X (strikethrough), E (code), . and , (superscript, subscript), \ (clear), K (link), Z, Shift+Z. At the edge of a link or code, Left and Right step in or out first. Try **bold**, `code`, # , - , 1. , > , or an address followed by a space. ?allowed=bold limits formats, ?rules=off turns input rules off.
